💡 Why Total Truck Mileage & Runs Are Lower in the Optimized Plan

1. Regional Hub Border Swapping (Stem Mile Elimination):

In naive planning, stores are strictly assigned to default primary hubs. For example, a GA/NC border store was dispatched 180 miles round-trip from Atlanta (`HUB_ATL_GA`), even though Raleigh (`HUB_RAL_NC`) was only 45 miles away. Dynamically swapping border stores eliminates 90–135 stem miles per border route.

2. Route Density & Stop Consolidation (26 Fewer Return Trips):

Naive routing ran 118 low-density truck runs with unoptimized empty legs. By consolidating 2–3 nearby store deliveries onto multi-temp 53ft trailers under weight/pallet limits, required truck runs dropped from 118 down to 92 (eliminating 26 redundant empty depot return trips).

Why This Solution Won: Mathematical Objective & Candidate Evolution

Explaining how the baseline plan was derived, the exact optimization objective, and why Candidate #28 was selected as the winning route plan.

1. How the Naive Baseline Plan Was Created

The baseline plan simulates standard manual logistics dispatching rules:

  • Fixed Primary Hub Allocation: Stores are strictly assigned to their default primary hub, regardless of whether a neighboring hub is 20+ miles closer.
  • Greedy Nearest-Neighbor Sequence: Orders are grouped using basic distance, ignoring delivery window start times.
  • Static 01:00 AM Dispatches: All trucks dispatch at 01:00 AM without adjusting for store key-drop window opening times (`01:00`–`05:00` or `06:00`–`09:00`).
  • Zero Supplier Backhauls: Trucks return empty to the regional hub after store deliveries, forfeiting $120/leg backhaul revenue credits.

2. Mathematical Objective Function

The objective function minimizes total net daily operational expense:

Goal: Minimize Total Daily Spend ($)
Daily Net Spend = [ Fuel + Driver Wages + Overtime + Maintenance + Tolls + Stop Fees ] – Backhaul Revenue Credits

Subject to strict zero-tolerance feasibility bounds:

  • Driver Shift Cap: Duration ≤ 11.0 Hours (660 mins max).
  • Trailer Capacity: Weight ≤ 42,000 lbs, Pallets ≤ 26.
  • Key-Drop Window: Arrival strictly inside store delivery window.
